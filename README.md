Kahnawake & Al Qider

Finding an Earth Location That Matches a Place on Mars

1. Project Overview

This project investigates the geological and environmental similarities between Kahnawake Crater on Mars and Al Qider Crater in Harrat Khaybar, Saudi Arabia.

The main goal is to study a selected location on Mars, analyze its features, and find a possible Earth analogue with similar characteristics.

By comparing both locations, the project explores how volcanic and desert environments on Earth can help us understand selected features of the Martian surface.

2. Study Areas

Mars: Kahnawake Crater

* Coordinates: 27.60°S, 156.90°W
* location : Mars.

Earth: Al Qider Crater, Harrat Khaybar

* Coordinates: 25.72°N, 39.94°E
* Location: Al Madinah, Saudi Arabia.

Both locations have similar absolute latitudes, although they are in opposite hemispheres.

3. Methodology

The project followed five main steps:

1. Select a Mars Location: Choose Kahnawake Crater as the study area on Mars.
2. Analyze Mars Data: Study its elevation, topography, slopes, rock types, dust, and other surface and environmental features.
3. Find an Earth Analogue: Search for a location on Earth with geological features similar to those found at Kahnawake Crater.
4. Analyze Earth Data: Study the elevation, topography, slopes, rock types, and environmental conditions of Al Qider Crater.
5. Compare Both Locations: Compare the results to identify similarities and differences between the two areas.

4. Data Sources and Tools

The project used several data sources and platforms:

* NASA Mars Trek: Used to explore the Martian surface and locate the study area.
* NASA Worldview: Used to examine Earth’s surface and volcanic features.
* MODIS Corrected Reflectance (Bands 7-2-1): Used to identify dark volcanic rocks and lava flows around Al Qider.
* NASA POWER: Used to study environmental conditions.
* NASA SRTM GL1 (30m): Used to analyze Earth’s elevation and terrain, accessed through OpenTopography.

5. Key Findings and Comparative Analysis

The comparison between Kahnawake Crater and Al Qider Crater covered eight geological and environmental aspects.

5.1 Geographic Location

* Kahnawake: 27.60°S, 156.90°W.
* Al Qider: 25.72°N, 39.94°E.

Comparison: Both locations have very similar absolute latitudes, although they are in opposite hemispheres.

5.2 Rock Types

* Kahnawake: CRISM data (FRT0000987B) identified pyroxene-rich basaltic rocks.
* Al Qider: MODIS images (Bands 7-2-1) showed dark basaltic rocks and scoria.

Comparison: Both locations show dark volcanic basaltic materials.

5.3 Volcanic Activity

* Kahnawake: An ancient crater with volcanic deposits and lava flows that were later covered by Martian dust.
* Al Qider: A volcanic field formed by successive basaltic lava flows in an arid environment.

Comparison: Volcanic activity is a major factor in shaping the surface features of both locations.

5.4 Humidity and Aridity

* Kahnawake: A hyper-arid environment with no liquid water and widespread dusty deposits.
* Al Qider: A hyper-arid desert environment no vegetation and surface water.

Comparison: Both locations have extremely dry conditions .

5.5 Elevation

* Kahnawake: The crater floor is at approximately 812 m, while the western rim reaches 1,230 m above the Martian zero-elevation level (MOLA).
* Al Qider: The surrounding plateau reaches approximately 1,300 m, while the crater rim reaches 1,632 m. The inner crater floor is approximately 1,520 m (SRTM).

Comparison: Both locations contain elevated crater rims and surrounding terrain, but their elevation profiles are different.

5.6 Slope

* Kahnawake: A gradual slope of approximately 6° (10.5%) leads toward a relatively flat crater floor.
* Al Qider: The surrounding plains have gentle slopes of 2°–7°, while the inner crater walls have steeper slopes of 18°–28°.

Comparison: Both locations have gentle slopes in parts of their terrain. 

5.7 Sky Clarity and Dust

* Kahnawake: Clear conditions with low dust levels (AOD 0.15–0.25) and slight seasonal dust activity, based on MGS TES data.
* Al Qider: Clear and stable atmospheric conditions, with an average clarity value of 0.68 over 11 years (NASA POWER, 2015–2025).

Comparison: Both locations show clear conditions and relatively low suspended dust in the studied data.

5.8 Solar Radiation

* Kahnawake: Average solar radiation of approximately 12.5 kWh/m²/day, with a solar angle of about 55°–60°.
* Al Qider: Average solar radiation of approximately 6.1 kWh/m²/day over an 11-year period (CERES).

Comparison: Both locations receive solar radiation, but their measured average values are different.

6. Overall Results

The analysis revealed several similarities between Kahnawake Crater and Al Qider Crater, especially in their dark volcanic materials, arid conditions, and some topographic features.

However, the comparison also showed important differences in elevation, crater shape, slope angles, and solar radiation.

These findings suggest that Al Qider could be a useful Earth analogue for studying selected geological features of Kahnawake Crater, although the two locations are not exact matches.

7. Project Impact

This project highlights the potential of geological locations in Saudi Arabia to support planetary research.

Studying Earth analogues can help researchers and space mission planners understand similar geological environments and investigate terrain conditions before future exploration missions.
